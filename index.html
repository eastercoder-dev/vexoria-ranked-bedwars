RBW RULEBOOK

Compete Fairly.

Vexoria Ranked Bedwars is built around competitive integrity, fair matchmaking and legitimate results.

01

Competitive Integrity

Cheating, prohibited modifications, exploiting, boosting and intentionally manipulating Ranked matches are prohibited.

02

Queue Conduct

Players joining the queue must be available and ready to participate in their assigned match.

03

Account Ownership

Account sharing, impersonation and attempts to evade Ranked restrictions are prohibited.

04

Match Conduct

Intentional throwing, cross-teaming or deliberate manipulation of a Ranked match result is prohibited.

05

Match Disputes

Scoring issues and match disputes should be reported with the relevant Game ID and evidence.

06

Enforcement

Ranked staff may apply appropriate penalties depending on the violation and its severity.
