CONDUCT RULES

Competition starts with clear standards.

Review conduct expectations, safety advisories and the enforcement ladders used across Vexoria Ranked Bedwars.

General Community Conduct

01 · Respect & Appropriate Behaviour

Treat all players and staff members with respect across Minecraft, Discord, and forums. Discrimination, hate speech, toxic conduct, harassment, doxxing, DDoS threats, causing drama, swearing, and rudeness are strictly forbidden.

02 · Competitive Integrity & Fair Play

Any modification, cheat, auto-clicker, macro, or exploit offering an unfair advantage over unmodded launchers is illegal. Stat boosting, stat farming, team griefing, illegal trapping, and teaming with cheaters can result in severe restrictions.

03 · Public Language Policy

All public Minecraft chat, including global and spectator chat, must be in English for active staff moderation. Non-English languages are permitted in private messages, party chat, and designated Discord channels.

04 · Punishment Evasion & Accounts

Using alternative accounts to play or chat while IP-banned or IP-muted is forbidden and can escalate the punishment. Account actions remain the responsibility of the account owner.

05 · Staff Interactions & Impersonation

Staff directives during matches, queue checks, or screenshares must be followed. Impersonating staff members or attempting to deceive staff is prohibited.

06 · Report & Evidence Integrity

Submitting falsified evidence, edited clips, misleading screenshots, or dishonest information in reports and appeals can result in severe network action.

Network Disclaimers & Safety Advisories

Account Sharing

We strongly discourage players from sharing accounts. Some punishments can include IP-based restrictions, so sharing may link your account to another player's enforcement history. It also creates account-security risks. Your account remains your responsibility.

Non-PC Gameplay

Playing from phones, tablets, or similar non-PC environments can create unusual movement or input patterns that may resemble prohibited gameplay. We strongly recommend using a standard PC Minecraft setup for Ranked.

VPN Usage

VPN usage is not automatically disallowed, but it is strongly discouraged. Shared or previously punished VPN exit IPs may cause your account to become associated with restricted addresses.
